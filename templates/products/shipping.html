Shipping Questions

Helpful information before you place your order.

When does the delivery period begin?

Transit time begins when the carrier accepts the shipment. Production and preparation happen before dispatch and should be considered separately.

Can I request expedited delivery?

Yes. Tell us your deadline and destination so we can review available carrier services and confirm applicable options and charges.

Can large orders be shipped to multiple locations?

Multiple destinations or split shipments may be possible. Contact us with the quantities and delivery addresses so we can assess the requirements.

How are shipping charges calculated?

Charges may depend on destination, package weight and dimensions, selected service, and handling or freight requirements. Live checkout prices should reflect the available carrier service and shipment details.

What if tracking information is unavailable?

Tracking may not appear immediately after a label is created. If it remains unavailable after dispatch, contact us with your order number.

Have Questions About Your Shipment?

Need urgent delivery, bulk freight, a multi-site shipment, or special job-site instructions? Send us your requirements so we can review available shipping options.
