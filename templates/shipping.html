How Shipping Works

Your order moves through production and fulfillment before it is released for delivery.

1
Order Received Your order or custom request is received and reviewed for the required product details.
2
Order Review Product specifications, quantities, artwork, and delivery requirements are confirmed.
3
Production Products are prepared, printed, finished, inspected, and packaged according to the order.
4
Dispatch Once the order is ready, it is handed to the selected delivery or freight service.
5
Delivery Your shipment is delivered to the address or delivery location provided with the order.
Important: Actual production and delivery timing depends on the product, quantity, artwork requirements, destination, shipping method, and order approval.

Delivery Options

The appropriate shipping method depends on your order size, destination, urgency, and delivery requirements.

Standard Package Delivery Suitable for regular product orders and smaller shipments.
Expedited Delivery Priority shipping may be available when a project requires faster delivery. Availability should be confirmed before ordering.
Commercial & Freight Delivery Larger orders, pallets, bulk decals, and commercial shipments may require freight or specialized delivery.
Multi-Location Delivery Commercial customers with multiple sites can request delivery arrangements for different locations.

Shipping FAQ

When does my delivery time begin? Delivery timing begins after the order is approved, produced, and released for shipment. Custom orders may require artwork approval before production.
Can I request urgent delivery? Yes. Tell us that your order is time-sensitive when requesting your quote or placing the order. Available expedited options depend on the destination and production schedule.
Can you ship large commercial orders? Yes. Large or heavy orders can be reviewed for commercial or freight delivery arrangements.
What if my package arrives damaged? Document the condition of the shipment and contact THE LABEL GROUP as soon as possible with your order information and photographs where applicable.
Can an order be shipped to a job site? Job-site delivery can be discussed for commercial orders. Provide complete delivery instructions when requesting the quote.
